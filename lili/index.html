Ready to put
in the work.

The toughest jobs need the toughest tools. Enter the Scout Terra with body-on-frame construction, solid rear axle, front and rear mechanical locking differentials, 4-wheel drive, and a 240V power outlet. More than a truck, the Scout Terra is the ultimate multi-tool. 1, 2

Go the distance,
and then some.

Pure electric models offer up to 350 miles of range. Scout’s extended range models offer 500 miles of range or more through a built-in, gas-powered generator. Even in the middle of nowhere, you can still get there. 8, 9

Leave nothing
behind.

Load up the heavy-duty power tools. The mountain bikes. The camping gear. Your friend’s couch. The extra dozen bags of groceries. Whatever else needs hauling. With a 5.5 ft bed plus ample storage throughout, everything can come along for the ride. 3

Rooted in heritage. Retooled for tomorrow.

The traits that made the original an icon never left. Bold, rugged, resilient. Just like those who drove them. The Scout Traveler and Terra were forged by that very spirit but retooled with modern ingenuity to keep drivers and passengers comfortable on, and off, the road ahead.

Exterior

  • Retractable rear window

    Breeze through

    Windows down. Roof open. Rear window retracted. Let the open-air breeze right in from everywhere.

  • 240-volt onboard power

    Power whenever, wherever

    When you need a charge, turn the truck into a power station. The offered 240-volt onboard power outlet, up to 6x USB Type C Ports, and 4x 120V outlets brings the comforts of home when you’re the furthest from it — at the jobsite, campsite or weekend game site.

  • Signature face

    A bold look from every angle

    Scout trucks have always made an impression that lasts. Inspired by the original, our new vehicles combine the workhorse design of the first Scout with a modern look. Iconic even from miles away.

  • NACS charging port

    The new standard

    The Scout Traveler and Terra debut with a North American Charging Standard (NACS) charge port that provides access to a wide range of charging stations across the country. Or, with an adaptor, use the Combined Charging System (CCS) for even more options.

  • Goldilocks 5.5 ft bed

    The ideal size

    We made it just right for the everyday. 5.5 ft is the ideal size bed for the heavy-duty tools and heavier-duty toys you need to tackle any job.

Interior

  • Real controls

    The feel of real

    Real knobs and real buttons. For precision and control that just clicks. Some things are just better in real life.

  • Built to outlast

    Rugged yet comfortable

    Inside and out, our vehicle’s materials are designed with durability and comfort in mind. Made to last and feel inviting the entire time.

  • The bench seat

    All in on togetherness

    The front bench seat is back and bringing extra copilots along for the ride with seating for up to six. Let loved ones and kids slide closer as everyone bounds down the road together. Or just enjoy that extra space all to yourself.

  • Inspired design

    Uncompromising style and comfort

    Scout trucks know how to make an impression. Our new models are inspired by the original Scout designs, combining their daring aesthetic and rugged exterior with a sleek, comfortable, modern cabin.

  • Community UX™

    Connect to what matters most

    No more competing for the AUX. Our digital ecosystem is designed for sharing. Scout Community UX lets your copilot take control of the screen, while you focus on the road ahead.
