Designed in Motor City

Scout vehicles are designed and engineered in Novi, Michigan. With a deeply rooted automotive tradition that attracts highly skilled engineers, Detroit serves as the creative and technical family hearth of Scout Motors. Our team understands innovation, advances with customer-first principles, and pulls storied Scout capability and DNA into the future.

Built in Blythewood

Blythewood, South Carolina is the home of all Scout vehicle manufacturing. The 1,100 acre production site adjacent to I-77 is home to a vibrant community of builders and doers that are in the surging epicenter of southeastern auto manufacturers and suppliers. With a workforce of 4,000 or more manufacturing employees, Scout Motors will continue to build opportunity and economic development for the community.

Showing up across America

We’re here for the long run, and as we expand, we’ll stay true to our roots. We work and build with pride, and that sentiment doesn’t stop once a Scout vehicle lands in a driveway. Through a national network of Scout Motors retail locations and dedicated customer support, you and your vehicle will always have Scout Motors by your side.

2022 Scout Motors is born.

Original Scout vehicles were born out of a desire to create a truck that could go anywhere and do anything— a true pioneer in the automotive world. In 1960, the first Scout, a Scout 80, rolled off the assembly line, setting the standard for what would become a capable workhorse that could also perform daily family duties. Built for durability and versatility, the Scout was embraced by adventurers, farmers, and outdoor enthusiasts alike, becoming a symbol of freedom and rugged independence. That same spirit lives on today in the new Scout Motors and in all Scout products.

A Community of Pioneers

Just as the first Scout vehicles appealed to the bold and pioneering, our growing community is composed of kindred spirits, and today adventure means a lot of things to a lot of people. Our community is filled with climbers, campers, foragers, and explorers.

They’re cooks, builders, athletes, and DIY experts. They’re thrill-seekers, city dwellers, adventure seekers, and modern makers. But the thing they all have in common is their connection to the land around them, their desire to create and support community, and an unyielding will to do the next good thing.

Looking Ahead

The road ahead is full of possibilities. As we move into the future, Scout Motors remains committed to innovation, sustainability, and the community of supporters who have held the torch. Our new line of Scout vehicles is just the beginning.

Startup speed and agility
New opportunities are rare. Starting with a clean sheet even rarer. Scout Motors is seizing this fresh start to design, engineer, build, and retail vehicles with customer-first obsession.
Backed by an industry titan
Automotive manufacturing is not for the faint of heart. The challenge, however, becomes less daunting with the backing of Volkswagen Group. Their manufacturing prowess is leveraged in Blythewood, South Carolina to produce high quality Scout vehicles.
Growing a broad array of talent
Innovation, humility, and customer obsession will always find a home at Scout Motors. Growing to over 600 employees in its first 24 months, Scout Motors is working at record speed. Whether in production, engineering, or retail, growth is our trajectory.
Direct customer relationships
The best relationships are rooted in trust. We’re establishing one-on-one relationships with our customers by selling every vehicle directly to the consumer.
