The one that
started it all.

The original off-road icon. The one that united work with play. The one that made “sport utility” a household term. The one to combine a farm tool with a family hauler, and from which all others have followed since. The spirit of the original, retooled and ready for a classic American comeback.

Our story
1961

Before 1961, International Harvester built tools that helped build, feed, and defend America. Then they shifted gears, producing sport utility vehicles that soon found a place in American driveways. The grit that defined the first generation of tractors and tools for the field translated seamlessly into a new era. That same ruggedness still drives Scout Motors today.
